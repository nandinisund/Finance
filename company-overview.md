# Bean & Barrel Coffee Co. – company overview (illustrative)

Bean & Barrel is a specialty coffee chain with 7 company-operated locations. Revenue is driven by walk-in traffic and average ticket; catering is a small add-on at University.

## Locations and opening dates
| Location | Opened | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Downtown | 2019-04-01 | Flagship, street-front |
| Airport | 2020-11-01 | Highest revenue, airside concession |
| University | 2021-08-15 | Seasonal with the academic calendar |
| Harbor | 2022-05-01 | Tourist traffic in summer |
| Midtown | 2022-10-01 | Office district |
| Suburban Mall | 2023-06-01 | Depends on mall anchor traffic |
| Riverside | 2025-03-15 | Originally planned for 2025-02-01; opening slipped after a permit hold |

## Conventions
- Budgets are set for full calendar months, even for a location's opening month.
- Rent is fixed by lease and should match budget every month.
